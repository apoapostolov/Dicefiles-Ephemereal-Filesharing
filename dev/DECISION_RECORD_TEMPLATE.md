# Decision Record

Copy this file to `docs/decisions/YYYY-MM-DD-SHORT-NAME.md`. Record decisions
that constrain future architecture or operations; routine implementation
details belong in code and tests.

## Title

Short decision name.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by `<link>`

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or constraint required a durable decision?

## Decision

What approach was chosen, including affected boundaries and invariants?

## Alternatives Considered

- Alternative and why it was rejected.

## Consequences

- What becomes simpler?
- What becomes harder or riskier?
- Are migration or compatibility steps required?

## Validation

Tests, runtime checks, measurements, or operational evidence that support the
decision.

## Revisit When

Concrete trigger that would justify reconsidering the decision.
